DegradX Experiment Log — v2

The second experiment programme, run under the brief “DegradX v2” on top of the frozen v1 results. It located where MATR's transfer gap sits, gave the generator per-unit offsets and correlated noise, tried a third dataset that stopped at its declared rule, and added two attribution scores. It also ran TimeSHAP at its full declared scale and piloted an observation-level target. Every methodological change made along the way is recorded.

Flags for the authors

These items need a decision or a revision by the authors. None of them was settled by the agent.

  1. The AI disclosure understates what the AI agent did. The Acknowledgments of paper/paper.tex say that Anthropic Claude Opus 5 was used “for the purposes of readability improvement, proofreading, and assisting with code implementation”. In both v1 and v2, an AI agent ran the experiments and drafted the declarations, the decision records (D25–D31 in v2) and the methodology and results text. The agent did not edit the acknowledgments, which are on the brief's no-touch list. The authors should revise the disclosure.
  2. The ISU-ILCC stop report awaits your decision. artifacts/v2/STOP_REPORT_third_profile_offsets.md recommends option A: report ISU-ILCC as the X6 outcome and continue with MATR and HUST. The X6-pending paper edits encode option A. They are in §3.4, with the related rows in §1, §3.1.1 and §3.3 of AMENDMENTS round 7, and they change if another option is chosen. Option B (condition-level mappings) would be a new D-record plus about 6–8 h of compute.
  3. The D28 E2 reading is a choice. ISU-ILCC passes E2 only under the declared-extraction branch: the level is tied to the C/5 measurement at the anchors. Under the strict reading, a direct full-capacity measurement at every position, E2 fails, and brief §6 (stop and report) would apply. See D28.
  4. The Abstract and Conclusion are still template placeholders. The abstract environment is empty, and the Conclusion reads “Your conclusion here”. The brief excluded both from the agent's edits. The authors write them against the v2 results.
  5. Several rules were set after data were seen. v1: D01, D04, D05, D13 and D20. v2: D27 and D30. The r3 declarations themselves were written after the v1 results were known (D25: “with every v1 result known”). The Limitations paragraph of §5 now states each of them in words (no IDs), adds the choice of HUST as the anchor, and says that the declarations for the second set of measurements were written after the first results were known. The authors should confirm that the wording is the one they want to publish.
  6. The discriminator cells of Table 1 are void under D02b readability (T6). In each profile only one of the three generation seeds is readable. The X3 reading “narrows” is a shift of the seed mean, not a readable level. v1's S5 cells were not re-checked: v1's predictions were not saved.
  7. v1's TimeSHAP comparisons mixed window sets. TimeSHAP was scored on 40 windows, IG and occlusion on 120. On matched windows, MATR's TimeSHAP rank agreement lies between IG and occlusion: its gap to IG is 0.021, against 0.083 on the mixed sets. V7 adds like-for-like rows. v2 runs every method on all 120 windows.
  8. The within-unit correlation estimator has a known errors-in-variables bias. ρ̄ is estimated from residuals about φc(ẑ), with ẑ read from noisy capacity, so correlations on the capacity pairs are inflated. The generator inherits the bias, and re-estimation on generated data adds it again (V3 anomaly 4, V4 warning 2, V5 anomaly 4). It is reported as a limitation, and the estimator is unchanged.
  9. X3 (correlated noise) has a negligible share of its own in the two measures where it was isolated (covariance agreement, discriminator). The X3-off set reaches the same values there, so those gains are X2's. Transfer and the property table were not run with X3 off, so X3's share in them is not measured. D29's cost line records this as a negative result for X3.
  10. v1's S6–S8 intervals used 2 000 bootstrap resamples, not 10 000 (T4). Whether any v1 reading sits within the extra Monte Carlo error of its threshold has not been re-checked. Every v2 interval uses 10 000.

  • MATR's v1 transfer gap sat in the non-capacity channels. Constant per-cell offsets explain about half of it, and the initial-capacity spread none (D26).
  • The generator with offsets and correlated noise (X2 + X3) brings HUST's transfer interval to contain 1 (1.17 [0.94, 1.34]) and MATR from 3.42 to 1.49. The covariance gap closes on both profiles; with X3 off it closes the same way, so that gain is X2's.
  • Every weighted channel is used by every ensemble member on both profiles and weightings. The accuracy and leakage gates pass.
  • The reference model is exact for IG, occlusion and TimeSHAP at full scale (TimeSHAP ≤ 2.5e-5). The D23 40-window subset means lie inside the full 120-window intervals on the primary weighting.
  • The temporal profile error registers end-loading and blur that rank agreement does not, and the allocation error registers mass moved between channels.
  • Full-scale TimeSHAP is feasible: 5 760 explanations in 1.71 h on 12 CPU workers, with exact equivalence.

Void or unsupported

  • The discriminator cells of Table 1: only one seed per profile is readable under D02b (T6).
  • Method orderings on trained models, except MATR channel allocation, where occlusion is worse. No score orders the methods on HUST.
  • Position-level identifiability on yobs (X9): the rank and temporal ranges exceed the method gap.
  • ISU-ILCC as a third profile: stopped at V4 (offset share 0.76–0.82). Regeneration is not shown at scale.
  • MATR inside HUST's transfer interval (X2(i)).
  • An own share for X3 in transfer or the property table: not measured.
  • The sparse set: no v2 profile enables inserted patterns. The operating range was not run (D25).
