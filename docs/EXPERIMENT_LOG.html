DegradX Experiment Log

What was run, in the order it was run: three measured battery datasets turned into fitted profiles, a generator with exact attribution ground truth, and reference values for three published attribution methods — with every methodological change made along the way.

Each stage below records what ran, what came out, which methodological problems were settled against the paper as written, and what the stage licenses. A measurement whose unit or event count falls below its declared minimum is reported as void rather than as a poor result — that convention is used throughout.

S0

Declarations, before any data was fitted

15 Sepconfigs/declarations.yaml r24 corrections, paper rounds 1–2

Every constant that the paper leaves open was written down first — weights, thresholds, window length, seeds, minimum counts — so that later fits could not be tuned to a result. Reading the methodology closely at this point exposed four places where the paper as written could not be implemented as stated. All four were fixed before a single profile was fitted.

C1The decomposable target could not be reproduced by any map of the window

Problem
With separate weights on the mean component and on the inserted patterns, a channel that carries both returns the wrong sum, and a pattern-carrying channel with zero graded weight still carries its mean component.
Decided
One weight per cell about a declared reference point: y = Σ w (m − x⁰) + Σ w p, with w = κc·πu and x⁰ the pristine level.
Cost
The sparse set now carries the position profile too, so under final-position weighting most inserted patterns have no ground-truth contribution. Buys an exact reference model.

C2Zero-weight channels still carry the degradation state

Problem
The declared leakage test — permutation importance near zero on every zero-weight channel — cannot pass, because every channel is a function of one state, and it cannot separate leakage from benign redundancy.
Decided
Two constructed null channels per profile carry the gate; redundant channels are reported instead. The reference model becomes the primary scoring target, and an ensemble of ten equally accurate models bounds what a trained-model score can mean.
Cost
Ten models per profile and weighting to train and attribute; two generated-only channels with no measured counterpart.

C3The degradation state put EOL below where cycling stopped

Problem
Taking ρ against the first observed capacity puts the threshold below the stopping rule of MATR and HUST, and early capacity rise makes the state negative at the start.
Decided
ρ is taken against documented nominal capacity and the early-life reference q₁ is the maximum over the first 20 positions: z = (q₁ − qt) / (q₁ − ρ·qnom).
Cost
Nominal capacity has to be verified per dataset; the state needs the first 20 positions before it is defined.

C4Attribution baselines were unstated

Problem
Shapley- and path-based attributions sum to the difference between the model output on the window and on a baseline; scoring them against the ground truth presumes the output is zero there.
Decided
The declared baseline is the pristine window, where the target is zero by construction. Ground truth for any other baseline is supplied, so methods keep their defaults; TimeSHAP is also run from its conventional average event as a sensitivity readout.
Cost
One extra TimeSHAP pass per profile.

The paper's construction needed four repairs to be implementable at all; each is recorded with its cost, and none of them was chosen after seeing a result.

S1

Data acquisition and ingestion

15 SepBatteryML @2861ae3b~12 min ingestion, CPU

MATR and HUST were downloaded and ingested through BatteryML, one MATR batch per process; NASA PCoE, which BatteryML does not cover, went through a converter validated against the raw arrays (maximum difference 0). Per-cycle channels — capacity, charge time, mean discharge voltage, internal resistance, mean temperature — were derived into one cycle table per dataset, with SHA-256 checksums for every raw file.

D11Which capacity series is the capacity channel

Investigated
Cycler-reported capacity against capacity integrated from current and time; they differ systematically on HUST.
Decided
The cycler-reported per-cycle capacity, on all three datasets, with the integrated series kept as a diagnostic.
Cost
HUST's source field had to be attached outside BatteryML, which drops it.

Ingestion is reproducible from checksums and one command; nothing here is a measurement yet.

D12 · D13 · D15 · D16 · D17Five rules the paper leaves implicit

Investigated
Isolated glitch cycles inflating noise estimates; records that stop within a hair of the threshold; an EOL search that started before the early-life reference; a residual fitted over positions the trajectory model does not cover.
Decided
A single-reading glitch rule for capacity and for the other channels (5 % isolated excursions), EOL credited for records ending within 0.01·qnom of the threshold, the EOL search starting at q₁, and the residual fitted from q₁ to EOL with runs capped at the smoothing window.
Cost
Each is an added rule in §3.3 of the paper; without them MATR noise variance was inflated 25× and one NASA unit had T = 1.

MATR and HUST are long, smooth and well-populated; NASA PCoE is the only dataset with real capacity regeneration and the only one too small to support fidelity measurement. That asymmetry decides everything downstream.

D04Family selection when the fit errors are indistinguishable

Investigated
On NASA PCoE, rollover had the lowest CV error but held its transition width at the optimiser's bound in 6 of 8 units — a fit that cannot be identified from the data.
Decided
A family is eligible only if its parameters are determined in at least 80 % of units; among eligible families the simpler one wins within a 5 % margin.
Cost
The NASA profile has no knee. The rule was added after the first fit, which is recorded; its evidence is the reproduction of EOL (p90 error 26 % vs 9.6 %).

D05When a pattern type is real rather than noise

Investigated
The declared detector fires on all three datasets, but the question is whether it fires more often than it would on pure noise with the same fitted variance and autocorrelation.
Decided
A pattern type is enabled only above twice the noise-only rate. MATR and HUST come in below their own noise rate (0.85×, 0.47×); NASA PCoE is 6.8× above it.
Cost
Only one profile carries inserted patterns, so every sparse-set measurement in the programme rests on NASA PCoE.

The profiles are fitted, but two of the three cannot exercise the sparse half of the benchmark at all — a consequence of the data, made explicit by a rule rather than by a choice.

S4

Generator and attribution ground truth

15 Sep3 generation seeds × 300 units162 checks pass

Each profile generates units whose every value is recorded by component: mean component, inserted patterns, noise. The decomposable target and its ground truth follow from those components, so the answer key is exact rather than estimated. The checks verify it: the ground truth sums to the target to 2.2 × 10⁻¹⁶ on every window, and the reference model reproduces the target exactly at zero noise.

D01Retrieval of the sparse set scored nothing

Investigated
Ranking the cells of one attribution map by magnitude recovers the inserted patterns barely above chance (AP 0.064 against 0.048) — even for the exact ground truth, because the mean component dominates every cell.
Decided
Each window ships with its pattern-free counterpart; retrieval is scored on the difference of the two maps, and single-map retrieval is kept as a secondary series.
Cost
Two attribution runs per window on the profile with patterns.

D18Generated units failed their own state check

Investigated
The state of a generated NASA unit started at 0.087 instead of 0, because its first position is drawn from a trajectory whose early-life reference differs from the profile's.
Decided
A generated unit's state is anchored at its own trajectory's early-life reference, the same rule the measured side uses. The check was not relaxed.
Cost
Regeneration of all units and one extra sensitivity refit.

From here the benchmark has what it promises: sequences with a known decomposition, a target that is exactly decomposable, and a model that attains it.

D02bOne minimum count for every measure was too coarse

Investigated
With five held-out units the discriminator gets a single test unit and the bootstrap interval of the representation distance is wider than the value itself.
Decided
A minimum per measure — 20 units reaching EOL for transfer, 12 for covariance, 8 for the discriminator, and a readability test on the interval for the representation distance.
Cost
NASA PCoE goes void on every fidelity column, and MATR's representation distance with it.

D19Could NASA PCoE be rescued by cross-fitting?

Investigated
Refitting the profile over five folds of all 18 units that define a state, to pool 10 held-out units reaching EOL.
Decided
No. The pooled ratio is 1.22 [1.06, 3.07] — an interval wider than its value — and the folds select three different trajectory families. NASA PCoE is reported as void on fidelity.
Cost
The one profile with inserted patterns has no evidence that it reproduces its dataset; this is stated wherever its results appear.

A profile can match a dataset in drift, length, family and noise and still fail the test that matters — MATR's charge time is set by per-cell protocol offsets the generator's shared mappings cannot express.

D21The inserted patterns are too small to be learned

Investigated
At the amplitudes fitted from NASA PCoE the pattern term carries 0.04 % of the variance of the mean term, and retraining without it leaves the error unchanged: −0.0000 [−0.0004, 0.0003]. A harness swept amplitude multipliers ×1 to ×16.
Decided
Keep the fitted amplitudes. Sparse-set scores on trained models are declared void, and the amplitude at which the term becomes learnable (×4, still inside the measured range) is reported as a generator setting rather than made the default.
Cost
The benchmark cannot currently evaluate whether a method recovers regeneration that a trained model actually uses. Choosing ×4 instead would have fitted the benchmark to its own check.

The graded field is learnable everywhere and leak-free; the sparse set is real in the data but negligible in the target, which is a finding about the datasets, not a defect of the construction.

S7

RQ3 — do the scores distinguish better maps from worse

15–16 Sep3 min + 32 min57 generator-setting values

Part one degrades the ground-truth map itself in controlled amounts — noise, mass shifted to either end, smoothing, permutation — and asks what the scores register. Part two varies the generator settings that control difficulty and asks where the scores stop being informative.

D08Two scores did not respond — was that a bug?

Investigated
Whether the flat response to smoothing and to shift-to-end came from the implementation or from the construction of the field.
Decided
From the construction: under recency weighting the graded field already increases toward the end of the window and varies smoothly along it, so neither operation reorders its cells. The resolution table now reports the size of the change beside the magnitude that is resolved.
Cost
A known blind spot, stated in the Results and the Discussion rather than patched.

D20On which model is the operating range read?

Investigated
The paper fixes the saturation rule but not the probe. On the trained model alone, all 57 examined setting values count as responsive — including two where the model is below the accuracy gate and its scores are void.
Decided
Both probes, plus the gate: a setting is inside the range only where the score is neither saturated nor at chance on the reference model and on the trained model, and the model is accurate enough to be scored. This excludes 8 of 57 values instead of none.
Cost
Range boundaries near the saturation level are ragged and are marked, not smoothed; paired retrieval turns out to have no operating range at all.

The scores work where they are meant to, and the two places where they do not are now documented: rank agreement ignores blur and end-loading, and paired retrieval is saturated everywhere.

D23TimeSHAP at its default configuration exceeded the compute budget

Investigated
At 5–7 s per window explanation, the declared design needs about 14 GPU-hours for TimeSHAP alone — more than twice the planned budget for the whole stage.
Decided
Reduce replicates only: a stratified 40 of the 120 windows, two sampling seeds on trained models and one on the reference model, where seed-to-seed variation is 3 × 10⁻⁷. Profiles, weightings, method configurations and the seed separation were left untouched.
Cost
TimeSHAP rows rest on a third of the windows and have wider intervals; the exact ceiling is recomputed on the same subset so the comparison stays like-for-like.

The distance from the ceiling to a trained model (0.26–0.57) dwarfs the distance between methods (≤ 0.089), and the ensemble spans more than that difference — so these are reference values for a method on a model, not a ranking of methods.

D24Averaging the reference values over profiles hid the measurement

Investigated
The declared table reported each method averaged over profiles. The averaged ordering of the three methods matches the profile's own ordering in only 4 of 9 profile × weighting combinations, the ensemble range is per profile (0.094 to 0.332), and retrieval exists in one profile only.
Decided
Report per profile, with the exact ceiling, the ensemble range and the average-event background as rows of the same block.
Cost
The table is three times longer, and later work must quote reference values per profile — which is what the measurement supports.

D10Two citations did not support the claims made from them

Investigated
The detection-family paragraph described one cited method as decomposition-based; the full text shows it predicts regeneration from rest time. A second citation was used for a claim about temperature as a model input that no accessible version of it makes.
Decided
The first was re-described from the source; the claim resting on the second was removed.
Cost
Temperature's role now rests on dataset evidence alone.

What the programme establishes, and where it stops

The construction holds in all three profiles: the ground truth is exact, the reference model attains it, and the target is learnable. What differs between profiles is how much of a dataset each reproduces and which half of the benchmark it can exercise.

Established

  • Exact, per-cell attribution ground truth on generated sequences calibrated to three measured datasets.
  • Reference values for TimeSHAP, IG and occlusion on a known function and on a trained model.
  • Score resolutions, blind spots, and the generator settings over which the scores stay informative.
  • HUST transfers at 1.46; a model learns nearly as much from the profile as from the cells.

Void or unsupported

  • NASA PCoE fidelity: 3 held-out units reaching EOL against a minimum of 20.
  • Sparse-set scores on trained models: the pattern term is not used at fitted amplitudes.
  • MATR representation distance: interval wider than the value.
  • Paired retrieval as a discriminating score: saturated at every setting examined.

Open for the author

  • The Conclusion section, left untouched by design.
  • The repository and DOI footnote in §1 — the only remaining placeholder.
  • Whether to keep MATR as a headline profile given its transfer ratio of 3.42.
