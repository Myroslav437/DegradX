DegradX Experiment Log — v2.1

The third experiment programme, run under the brief “DegradX v2.1” on top of the frozen v2 results. It added a third evaluated profile, FORM, built from the TRI/Stanford formation dataset of Cui et al. (2024). FORM is the first profile whose generator inserts patterns. The one MATR and HUST computation of this programme measured the share of the correlated noise in transfer. The programme also delivered a review build of the paper in which every change since the authors' text is highlighted. ISU-ILCC stays closed under option A.

3 profiles: MATR, HUST, FORM FORM: 182 cells, all reach EOL 4 680 TimeSHAP explanations decisions D32–D34 amendments round 8 tags v2-results, declarations-frozen-r4
RQ1 · fidelity (FORM) 1.54 [1.28, 1.74]

FORM's transfer ratio. It lies outside HUST's interval [0.94, 1.34], as MATR's does, but the intervals overlap. Unlike MATR's, the gap is located: in the non-capacity channels, mostly in their early-life levels. FORM's discriminator cell is the first readable one (0.10–0.17).

RQ2 · usability (FORM) 3 of 3 · 0.0000

Every weighted channel is used materially by every member (NRMSE 0.179). The pattern term is separable (|r| 0.045) but not shown to be learned: removing it leaves the error at 0.0000 [−0.0001, 0.0002], a test that cannot detect a term this small. So the trained-model sparse-set scores are void (D21 logic, no inflation).

RQ3 · scores 1.00 → 0.37

Paired retrieval of the undegraded map, then under the smallest added noise, measured for the first time. FORM reproduces the registering pattern of MATR and HUST exactly: rank agreement misses smoothing and partial shifts, and the temporal profile error registers them.

Transfer shares (MATR / HUST) 2.19× / 1.30×

The part of the v1 → v2 transfer gain carried by the per-unit offsets. Correlated noise carries 1.02× / 0.98×, i.e. none: the gains of v2 are the offsets' in every measure where the two were separated.

Flags for the authors

These need a decision or a revision by the authors. The Notes box on the first page of paper/review/DegradX_review.pdf lists the same items, with pointers into the paper. None was settled by the agent.

  1. Licence. The FORM profile and its generated units inherit CC BY-NC 4.0, a non-commercial term. The README says so. The cell-to-protocol sheet (the authors' GitHub file, no licence) is fetched and pinned, never redistributed.
  2. Nominal capacity of FORM: 0.24 Ah, from Cui et al. p.3082 (“nominally 240 mAh … 1C is defined as 0.24 A”). BEEP's stored nominal_capacity agrees.
  3. HUST stays the anchor (D29, the brief's ruling), on transfer, covariance and the property table, not the discriminator. FORM's transfer ratio, 1.54, lies outside HUST's interval, as MATR's does. The agent reports this and does not re-decide the anchor.
  4. Post-data rules and declaration timing. v2.1 adds two post-data rules, both for FORM:
    • D33, the charge current of the protocol check read on constant-current samples. Read as declared, it would have stopped FORM on a false second protocol made by post-EOL collapsed cycles.
    • A post-hoc sensitivity of the mechanism diagnostic, which gives the same reading.
    r4 was written after every earlier result, and after FORM's documentation (with outcome-adjacent values) and the measured values of one of its cells had been read (all listed in v2_1.exposure), but before any download. All of this is in the Limitations, coloured.
  5. E3 = 66 corrects an off-by-one (67). Tongji is not re-opened.
  6. The D28 E2 reading for ISU-ILCC is still a choice. ISU-ILCC is closed and reported, not evaluated.
  7. FORM's sparse set. It exists, but no trained model is shown to learn it, so its retrieval is read on the reference model and on degraded maps only. Decide whether the paper presents it as a construction check only.
  8. The AI-use disclosure. The Acknowledgments are untouched. A proposed disclosure is in the Notes box: the experiments were run by an AI agent, which also proposed the decision records and drafted the methodology amendments and the results text.
  9. The Abstract and Conclusion are agent drafts, marked “DRAFT (agent) — for the authors to rewrite”. The repository and DOI footnote in §1 is still a placeholder.
  10. Table 1 layout. r4 lists four TSTR rows for MATR/HUST. The paper prints “v2 (three seeds)” as the main row and “v2 without X3 (seed 0)” and “v1” as rows. “v2 (seed 0)” goes into the note.
  11. 21 of 300 generated FORM units end above the EOL threshold by more than the declared record-end tolerance (2.4 mAh), because their capacity noise varies slowly. They are left out of the property rows that depend on EOL (D30 applied as declared).

D33The protocol check reads the charge current on constant-current samplespost-data

Found
Read as declared (the 90th percentile of all charge samples), 40 cells carry runs of more than 11 cycles with a departing charge current, agreeing in 114 pairs. That is a second aging protocol, which is a stop case. Every such cycle is long after EOL, with capacity at 0.006–0.046 Ah: the charge is almost all CV, so the percentile no longer measures the CC level. Discharge current and voltages are unchanged.
Chosen
The median current over CC samples (below the cycle's own Vcv − 10 mV, current above C/50, at least 10 samples). A cycle without a CC phase is not tested. Result: no cell out of tolerance, no second protocol.
Kept
The literal outcome is kept in ingest_summary_r4_literal.json, cells_r4_literal.csv and checks_r4_literal.md. Listed in the paper's Limitations and Notes box.

What the programme establishes, and where it stops

The construction holds on all three profiles, and FORM tests it on a third chemistry, format and laboratory. The benchmark now carries an inserted pattern type, but at its fitted amplitude no trained model is shown to learn it.

Established

  • FORM is eligible by the rules declared before download. It is learnable and every channel is used.
  • FORM's transfer gap (1.54) is located: in the non-capacity channels, mostly their early-life levels.
  • Correlated noise has no share in transfer (MATR 1.02×, HUST 0.98×). The offsets carry the v2 gains.
  • The covariance gap is closed on all three profiles.
  • The registering pattern of the scores is identical on a third profile. Paired retrieval works as designed on degraded maps.
  • On the reference model all three methods are exact on FORM too, and they localize its sparse set exactly (paired retrieval 1.00).

Void or unsupported

  • Trained-model sparse-set scores on FORM (pattern term not shown to be learned).
  • The transfer ratio of FORM with independent noise (declared for MATR and HUST only).
  • The discriminator cells of MATR and HUST (D02b).
  • MATR or FORM inside HUST's transfer interval.
  • Any method ordering on FORM's trained models: the ten-model floor exceeds every gap.

Open for the authors

  • The licence term (NC) for the release.
  • The post-data rules D33 and the mechanism sensitivity; the r4 timing wording.
  • Whether FORM's sparse set is presented as a construction check only.
  • The AI-use disclosure, the Abstract and Conclusion drafts, the repository footnote.
  • The candidate for the remaining gaps of MATR and FORM: channel trajectories whose shape differs between protocols. Mappings per protocol would test it, at the cost the offset bound governs.
